RULES & REGULATIONS

HIGHEST CARDS IN ORDER

  • 1. Big Joker (Black Joker)
  • 2. Lil Joker (Red Joker)
  • 3. Deuce of Spades

GAME OBJECTIVE

  • THE FIRST TEAM TO SCORE 250 POINTS IN 60 MINUTES WINS
  • If no one reaches 250 in 60 minutes, the team with the highest score wins
  • THE CHAMPIONSHIP GAME IS PLAYED TO 350 POINTS

DEALING & BIDDING

  • THE DEALER IS CHOSEN BY WHO PULLS THE HIGHEST CARD FROM DECK
  • BOARD / MINIMUM BID IS FOUR

UNDERBIDDING/SANDBAGGING

  • A team is penalized by 100 point deduction for accumulating 6 or more sandbags/overages during the course of the game

BLIND BIDDING

  • If a team is down 100 points they can blind bid a minimum of 6 books

THROWING YOUR HAND IN

  • Only permitted if no face cards or spades

GENERAL RULES

  • NO PASSING SPADES TO PARTNERS
  • IF IT'S PLAYED IT'S NOT LAID. A player can retract a card they have placed on the table, as long as they do so before the next player has played their card

SUBSTITUTE PARTNER

  • A SUBSTITUTE PARTNER IS ALLOWED IF A PLAYER HAS TO LEAVE THE TOURNAMENT
  • The new partner cannot be an active participant playing in the tournament (Aka The Sabrina Rule)

RENEGING

  • If you are caught reneging (not following the card suit when you have the suit) the other team gets three of your books
  • These books will not count as sandbags for the other team

NO CHEATING

  • NO CHEATING - No speaking over the tables
  • No touching the cards when down
  • No pointing
  • No saying allusive things with the intention to influence your partner's play
