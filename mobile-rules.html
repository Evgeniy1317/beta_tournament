Game Rules

🎯 Objective

Be the first team to reach 500 points by winning tricks and making accurate bids.

🃏 Card Dealing

Each player receives 13 cards. Dealer rotates clockwise after each hand.

📊 Bidding

Teams bid on how many tricks they think they can win. Bids range from 0-13.

♠️ Spades Rules

Spades are always trump. You must follow suit if possible, otherwise you may play any card.

📈 Scoring

10 points per trick won, plus 1 point per trick bid. Overtricks cost 1 point each.

🏁 Winning

First team to reach exactly 500 points wins. Going over 500 resets your score to 250.

Tournament Format

Round 1: Qualifying

All teams play 3 games. Top 8 teams advance to elimination rounds.

Round 2: Quarterfinals

Best of 3 games. Winners advance to semifinals.

Round 3: Semifinals

Best of 3 games. Winners advance to the final.

Final: Championship

Best of 5 games. Winner takes the $500 grand prize!
